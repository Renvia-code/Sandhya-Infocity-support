Before you set off

On the route

The campus keeps working while the walk is on. These few things keep everybody safe.

  1. Follow the rope line. It marks the approved route from start to finish.
  2. Do not enter the 110 kV substation area or any electrical yard. Follow the marshals where the route passes them.
  3. Give way to vehicles. The internal roads stay in use during the walk.
  4. The Thalambur road back gate stays closed while the walk is on. Enter and leave by the Main Gate.
  5. Stay off ramps, basements and service yards. They are not part of the route.
  6. Tell a marshal or the nearest security guard if you see a hazard, a dark stretch or anything unsafe.
